Rendering modes reference

  • Filter+: expanded inversion corrections for images, video, SVG, canvas, and backgrounds.
  • Filter: inversion-based darkening with basic media safeguards.
  • Dynamic: adaptive dark colors for layouts, surfaces, forms, and typography.
  • Static: consistent, predictable stylesheet with minimal runtime adaptation.

Privacy principles

  • No analytics, telemetry, or tracking is included.
  • All preferences and site lists are stored locally in browser storage.
  • No browsing history is ever uploaded or collected.
  • No remote code execution.
